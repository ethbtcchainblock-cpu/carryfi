How the carry trade works

Perpetual futures use a funding rate to keep their price anchored to spot. When funding is positive, longs pay shorts. By holding long spot + short perp in equal size, you're delta neutral — price moves cancel out — and you collect the funding payment every interval.

Example: $10,000 in BTC spot + $10,000 short BTC perp. If funding is 0.01%/h on Hyperliquid, you earn $10,000 × 0.01% = $1/hour, or $24/day, $720/month — with no exposure to BTC's price direction.
