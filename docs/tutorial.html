The Strategy

Delta neutral means price doesn't matter

You hold two equal and opposite positions. They cancel each other out — you profit from the funding payment, not from price movement.

Position 1 — SPOT

Buy XMR on spot

You own the actual coin. If price goes up, you profit here.

📈
Position 2 — PERP SHORT

Short XMR perp

Same dollar size. If price goes up, you lose here — exactly cancels spot profit.

📉
Net price exposure = $0.00  ·  You only collect the funding payment

Exiting the Trade

Close when the rate stops paying

CarryFi doesn't alert you when to exit yet — but here's the simple rule:

APR above 20% — stay in, keep collecting
APR between 5–20% — decide based on exchange fees
Rate goes negative — EXIT immediately, you'd be paying instead of collecting

To close the trade:

1. Close your short perp — buy back the same amount
2. Sell your spot position — sell the same XMR you bought
3. Your net P&L = just the funding you collected ✅
